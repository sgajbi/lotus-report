# Roadmap

## Current direction

- keep RFC-0002 portfolio review as a first-class, machine-readable front-office meeting-pack
  contract
- tighten repo onboarding around real reporting ownership and boundary rules
- keep reporting contract documentation aligned with current request conventions
- improve reporting payload clarity without inventing new domain ownership

## Watchlist areas

- future standardization of mixed request conventions
- cross-app drift between reporting composition and upstream contract evolution
- readiness of live aggregation versus placeholder/static aggregation paths
- authoritative suitability, mandate-guideline, target-allocation, tax-lot, and realized-gain/loss
  data needed for the next level of portfolio review completeness
- governed `lotus-ai` integration for grounded advisor assistance after source refs, approval
  controls, and blocked-use-case enforcement are in place
